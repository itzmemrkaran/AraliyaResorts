FAQ

Good to know

Are the rooms kid-friendly?

Yes. Family suites with separate rooms are available.

When is the best time to visit?

December to March suits the south-west and hill country. April to September suits the north and east.

What should I wear?

Light clothing on the coast, warm layers in Nuwara Eliya, and modest dress at temples.

Is the payment real?

No. This is a concept with a simulated gateway. Going live needs a provider such as PayHere or Stripe with server-side keys.
